Blockchain economy

Blockchain economy

The blockchain economy (on-chain economy) is an environment where assets and capital flows are digitized and operate directly on the blockchain. By removing intermediaries, transactions become faster, more transparent, and secure. Digital assets can be issued, distributed, and exchanged continuously, allowing capital to circulate more efficiently. This results in higher monetary velocity, enhancing the utility of capital and assets within the economy. At the same time, blockchain enables businesses to expand their borderless connections, reaching global markets and capital sources.
