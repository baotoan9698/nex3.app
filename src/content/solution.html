Web3 Reward Platform

A solution that connects reward budgets in cryptocurrency from Web3 projects to a business’s user base — enabling additional incentive programs, reducing marketing costs, increasing in-app engagement, and delivering new user experiences.

Enterprises integrate with the NEX3 reward ecosystem, which is supported by incentive budgets from Web3 projects

Users complete missions to earn NEX3 XP (experience point), which can be redeemed for rewards or used to level up.

NEX3 operates the platform, manages the reward pool, and maintains the Web3 infrastructure.

Enterprises act as distribution channels, bringing the experience directly to their users.

Solution Description

- Businesses connect to the NEX3 reward system (sponsored by Web3 projects). - Their users complete tasks → earn NEX3 Tokens or XP → redeem gifts or upgrade their membership level. - NEX3 operates the platform, manages the reward pool, and provides the Web3 infrastructure. - Businesses help distribute the experience to their own users.

Web3 Protocol-as-a-service

Web3 Protocol-as-a-service

NEX3 offers a full-stack Web3 protocol solution as a service (On-chain Protocol-as-a-Service), enabling businesses to easily launch on-chain applications without the need to build an in-house blockchain team.

Equipped with essential infrastructure — including a Web3 wallet, point accumulation engine, membership tiering, and token & NFT logic — businesses can:

  • Rapidly deploy on-chain experiences such as loyalty, membership, staking, and more;

  • Easily integrate with their existing digital systems (CRM, mobile app, POS, etc.);

  • Gradually transition into the On-chain Economy in a safe, risk-free manner.

NEX3 provides the complete Web3 loyalty backend — including wallet, NFTs, token engine, XP system, membership tiers, and more.

Businesses use it as an infrastructure layer to build their own loyalty tokens and customized reward programs.

Full control remains with the business — from rewards to data and UX.
